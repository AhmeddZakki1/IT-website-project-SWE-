Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I book a car?
    Select a vehicle, choose dates, add your details, and confirm payment.

  • Can I cancel my reservation?
    Yes. Cancellation terms depend on your selected rate plan.

  • What documents are required?
    A valid driver's license, ID/passport, and booking confirmation.

  • What if I return the car late?
    Additional hourly or daily fees may apply.
